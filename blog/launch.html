Pepper is public

2026-10-19

Pepper's source, its package repositories and its documentation are now public, under the pep-lang organization on GitHub.

Pepper is a compiled, memory-safe language with reference counting instead of a garbage collector, and with thread safety the compiler checks. The compiler, runtime and tools are written in Pepper itself.

To try it, install the toolchain and take the tour. Questions are welcome in Discussions, and bugs in Issues.

Pepper is open source under the Apache-2.0 license. Source, issues and discussions are on GitHub.
